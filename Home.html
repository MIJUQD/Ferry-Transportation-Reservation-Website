ABOUT US

The Balanacan-Dalahican Port, situated in Mogpog, Marinduque, Philippines, is an essential maritime entry point for the island, linking it to neighboring provinces via ferry transportation. Its history begins during the Spanish colonial era, when trade was its primary purpose. The port's development over time has made it an essential center for transportation, enabling the flow of both people and products and promoting Marinduque's socioeconomic growth and connection to the rest of the nation.
